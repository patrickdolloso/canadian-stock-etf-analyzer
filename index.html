The Canadian Stock & ETF Analyzer applies the asset-location principles from the Advanced Investor Kit: US-listed US equities in RRSP, CAD-listed/swap in TFSA, Canadian equity in Non-Reg.

Assumptions & caveats

  • Projections use illustrative expected returns (US eq ~7%, CDN eq ~6.5%, global ~6.8%, bonds ~3.5%) minus MER.
  • Tax drag = yield × applicable rate (US withholding 15%, CDN div tax credit ~refund, swap 0%). Educational estimates, not advice.
  • Non-resident TFSA 1%/month penalty not modeled here — covered in the full Kit.
  • Consult a cross-border tax professional before acting.
