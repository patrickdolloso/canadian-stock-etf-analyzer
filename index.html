🇨🇦 Built for Canadians, by a Canadian

The Canadian Stock & ETF Analyzer applies the asset-location principles from the Advanced Investor Kit: US-listed US equities in RRSP, CAD-listed/swap in TFSA, Canadian equity in Non-Reg.

Assumptions & caveats

  • Projections use an expected return you set (default 7%) minus MER and tax drag.
  • Tax drag = yield × withholding/income rate. US-listed US equities: 0% in RRSP, 15% in TFSA/Non-Reg. Swap ETFs: 0% distribution drag.
  • Non-resident TFSA penalty, rebalancing, and contribution-room limits not modeled — covered in the full Kit.
  • Consult a cross-border tax professional before acting.
